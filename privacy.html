Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version for parents

  • ✅ No sign-up, no forms, no ads. We never ask your child for a name, email, photo or any other personal information.
  • ✅ Stars stay on your device. The stars and learning streak are saved only in your own browser. We can't see them.
  • ✅ Videos play from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to the player.

1. Who we are

This website (learningwithfun-kids.web.app) is the home of Learning with Fun, the YouTube channel @LearningwithFunkid. It shows our learning videos for toddlers and preschool children (ABC, numbers, tables, shapes, rhymes and stories), grouped by topic. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Learning with Fun.

2. What information we collect

We do not collect personal information through this website. There are no accounts, logins, contact forms, comment boxes, newsletters, payments or advertising on the site.

Like almost every website, the company that hosts our pages (Google Firebase Hosting) automatically receives basic technical information when a page is loaded, such as the device's IP address, browser type and the time of the visit. This is used to deliver the website and keep it secure. We do not use it to identify anyone, and we don't add any analytics or tracking tools of our own.

3. Children's privacy

Our videos are made for young children, and this website is designed to be safe for them to use with a parent or carer nearby.

  • We do not knowingly collect, use or share personal information from children.
  • Children cannot create an account, send messages or post anything on this website.
  • Buttons that lead to YouTube or WhatsApp (such as "Like on YouTube", "Subscribe" or "Share") open another website or app. We encourage parents to use these buttons on their child's behalf.

If you believe a child has somehow given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

4. Information saved on your device

When your child taps "I learned it!", the website remembers which videos earned a star and which days you visited, so it can show the star count and learning streak. This is saved in your browser's local storage on your device only. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The website still works fine if you do.

We do not set any advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Services we use

To show videos and make the site work, your browser connects to these services. Each one handles data under its own privacy policy:

▶️ YouTube (Google)

Plays our videos and shows their pictures. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), so YouTube doesn't store viewing information until a video is played. Google Privacy Policy

🔥 Firebase Hosting (Google)

Delivers the website's pages. Firebase privacy

🔤 Google Fonts

Provides the friendly lettering on the site. Google Fonts privacy

💬 WhatsApp

Only if you tap "Share" and choose WhatsApp. WhatsApp Privacy Policy

6. Links to other websites

Our pages link to YouTube and other websites. Once you leave this site, the other website's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for how other websites handle information.

7. Your choices

  • Clear the stars saved on your device at any time in your browser settings.
  • Block or delete cookies set by YouTube or Google in your browser settings.
  • Manage your YouTube and Google data at myaccount.google.com.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights over your data. Since we don't collect personal information, we usually won't hold any about you, but you are always welcome to ask us.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the website works, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top.

9. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email us at learningwithfunkid@gmail.com.
